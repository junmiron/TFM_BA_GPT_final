# Functional Specification - AI Dialer Project

## Product Overview
The AI dialer presents opportunities to revolutionize customer engagement by automatically initiating calls offering travel packages. When customers accept the offer, the call is seamlessly transferred to a human agent; otherwise, the AI attempts convincing up to three times before termination.

### Integration Scope
**Scope In:**
- Integrated with call-center systems for calls & transfers.
- Connections with CRM systems to fetch customer profile data.
- Pre-defined databases ensuring calls efficiency.
- Save success feedback, RAG-answering queries & reports prepared.

**Metrics Summary:** Successful Calls & KPIs outlined through template setups by travel agencies.
